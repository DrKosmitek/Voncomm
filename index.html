Welcome to Voncomm Corporation

Voncomm Corporation has been building computing hardware and software for offices and businesses since our founding in 1984. From desktop workstations to networked terminal systems, we provide the tools that keep offices running day to day.

Our work covers office desktop computers, operating systems, and network infrastructure. We believe in straightforward, reliable systems that do not get in the way of the people using them.

What We Do

  • Office desktop computers for professional environments
  • In-house operating system development
  • Networked terminal systems for business use
  • Network consulting and office infrastructure support
  • On-site installation and maintenance
