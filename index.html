Welcome to Voncomm Corporation

Voncomm Corporation has been providing reliable networking solutions, terminal computing systems, and operating software since our founding in 1984. We are a privately held company dedicated to building the tools that keep networks running and terminals connected.

Our work is focused on three core areas: Rhodium workstations, Moriander OS, and networking. We believe in simple, stable, and well-documented systems.

What We Do

  • Rhodium desktop computers and professional hardware
  • Moriander operating system development and licensing
  • Legacy support for Iridium terminal systems
  • Network consulting and infrastructure support
  • On-site installation and maintenance
