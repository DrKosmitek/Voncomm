Welcome to Voncomm Corporation

Voncomm Corporation has been providing reliable networking solutions, desktop computing systems, and proprietary operating software since our founding in 1984. We specialize in building integrated environments where hardware and software work in perfect unison.

Our current focus is on the Rhodium Desktop powered by the Moriander OS. We continue to support our legacy Iridium terminal users while transitioning to modern graphical computing.

What We Do

  • Rhodium high-performance professional workstations
  • Moriander Graphical Operating System development
  • Legacy Iridium terminal system maintenance
  • Enterprise network consulting and infrastructure
  • On-site installation and hardware deployment
